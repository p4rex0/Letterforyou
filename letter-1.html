If you’re reading this

I still remember that day.

I was waiting for you at the metro station, and honestly, I was a little nervous. It was our first time seeing each other, and I had no idea how that moment was going to be.

I kept checking my phone again and again.

“Did she reach?”

“Is she here yet?”

And then, suddenly…

You appeared in front of me.

For a second, I just froze.

My mind went completely blank.

You were wearing that blue kurta, jeans, and the grey jacket. I looked at you and thought,

“Damn… she looks so pretty.”

I don't know if I told you that properly that day, but you really did look gorgeous.

Then you smiled at me.

And honestly, that smile got me.

My heart just melted.

Your eyes were shining, and I was already getting lost in them. I was so lost in my thoughts that I even fumbled a little. Then I tried to pull myself together.

You said,

“Hi.”

And I said,

“Hi.”

We side-hugged.

And inside, I was probably much more nervous than I looked.

I remember thinking,

“Okay… I'm actually meeting her. She's actually standing right in front of me.”

I was genuinely mesmerized by you.

Then we started going towards the café to have coffee. I was quiet at first, and you started talking.

And somehow, you made me feel comfortable.

You didn't even have to try too hard. You just talked to me, and slowly, I started feeling less nervous.

We talked on the bike, and then we finally reached the café.

When we sat across from each other, there was one thing I really wanted to do.

I wanted to sit beside you.

I wanted to be close to you.

But I was too shy to say or do anything about it.

So I just sat there, in front of you, pretending to be normal.

Meanwhile, in my head, I was just staring at you.

Admiring your face.

Especially when you were happy and smiling.

We kept talking…

And talking…

And somehow, I started getting to know you.

Little things about you. The way you talked. The way you reacted. The way you made me feel comfortable without even realizing you were doing it.

And somewhere in between all those conversations, I remember thinking,

“I don't want this to end.”

But time doesn't really care about what we want.

Our time was coming to an end.

Eventually, I dropped you off.

We looked at each other and said,

“Bye.”

And then you left.

But something about that day stayed with me.

That day became one of my best days.

Not because we went to a café.

Not because we talked for hours.

But because I met you.

I met someone who was genuinely kind and good to me.

I really loved the way you comforted me that day. You have this cozy, warm personality that makes people feel safe around you.

And I don't think you even realized how much that meant to me.

I went there feeling nervous about meeting you…

And I came back feeling like I had met someone I didn't want to lose.

And the funny thing is…

I didn't even realize when it happened.

I didn't know when I started falling for you.

I just know that somewhere between that first “Hi,” your smile, our conversations, and the way you made me feel comfortable…

I fell for you.
